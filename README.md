# Martian Shooter

**Play it now in your browser: https://loco306.github.io/martian-shooter/**

A first-person shooter that runs in any desktop browser, no install. Ten levels: five on the target range shooting spheres out of the sky, five fighting little green Martians who land in saucers, take cover, dodge your aim and shoot back. Five weapons, headshots, combos, and Martians that melt into green goo when they die.

Built entirely with Claude (Claude Code, Fable 5.1) in a single evening, as one HTML file plus Three.js. Media and the full build story are below.

## Controls
Mouse look · LMB fire · RMB aim (scope on the sniper) · R reload · 1-5, Q/E or mouse wheel to switch weapons · WASD move · Shift sprint · C crouch · F fullscreen · Esc pause

## Run it locally
Download `index.html`, `three.min.js` and `SkyShooter.ico` into one folder and open `index.html`. Works offline.
